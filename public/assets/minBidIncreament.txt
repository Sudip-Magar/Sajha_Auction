=====================================================================
MINIMUM BID INCREASE - HOW IT WORKS IN SAJHA AUCTION
=====================================================================

1. WHAT IS IT?
---------------------------------------------------------------------
On the live auction page you see a box like this:

    MINIMUM BID INCREASE
    + Rs. 1,000.00

It answers one question:

    "How much MORE than the current price must my next bid be?"

Example:
    Current standing price : Rs. 320,729.22
    Minimum bid increase   : Rs. 1,000.00
    -------------------------------------------------
    Lowest bid you may place = 320,729.22 + 1,000.00 = Rs. 321,729.22

If you try to bid less than that, the site rejects the bid with the
message "Your bid must be at least Rs. ...".


2. IS IT ALWAYS 1,000?
---------------------------------------------------------------------
NO. It is calculated. It only shows 1,000 when the price is high.

The number is NOT typed in by anyone for each bid. The site works it
out automatically from two things:

    (A) A price-based step   (built into the site, same for everyone)
    (B) The seller's own minimum increment (the seller types this when
        listing the auction; the default in the form is Rs. 100)

The final number is the BIGGER of the two:

    minimum bid increase = the larger of (A) and (B)


3. PART (A): THE PRICE-BASED STEP
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The site looks at the CURRENT STANDING PRICE and picks a step from this
table:

    +----------------------------------+-------------------+
    | Current standing price           | Price-based step  |
    +----------------------------------+-------------------+
    | less than Rs. 1,000              | Rs. 50            |
    | Rs. 1,000  to  Rs. 4,999         | Rs. 100           |
    | Rs. 5,000  to  Rs. 19,999        | Rs. 250           |
    | Rs. 20,000 to  Rs. 49,999        | Rs. 500           |
    | Rs. 50,000 or more               | Rs. 1,000         |
    +----------------------------------+-------------------+

Why does the site do this?
    - A cheap item (Rs. 800) should not need a Rs. 1,000 jump.
    - An expensive item (Rs. 300,000) should not be won by people
      bidding Rs. 10 higher each time. Auctions would drag on forever.
    So the step grows as the price grows.

So, 1,000 is simply the LARGEST step in the table. Any auction whose
price is Rs. 50,000 or more gets it.


4. PART (B): THE SELLER'S MINIMUM INCREMENT
---------------------------------------------------------------------
When a seller creates an auction, there is a field:

    "Min Bid Increment (Rs.)"

The seller can type any amount there. The site then compares it with
the price-based step and keeps the LARGER one.

IMPORTANT: the seller can only make the step BIGGER. The seller can
never make it smaller than the table above.


5. THE RULE, WITH EXAMPLES
---------------------------------------------------------------------

    minimum bid increase = MAX( price-based step , seller's increment )

    Example 1
        Current price      : Rs. 8,000
        Price-based step   : Rs. 250       (price is in 5,000-19,999)
        Seller's increment : Rs. 100
        Result             : MAX(250, 100)   = Rs. 250

    Example 2
        Current price      : Rs. 30,000
        Price-based step   : Rs. 500       (price is in 20,000-49,999)
        Seller's increment : Rs. 300
        Result             : MAX(500, 300)   = Rs. 500

    Example 3  (this is your screenshot)
        Current price      : Rs. 320,729.22
        Price-based step   : Rs. 1,000     (price is 50,000 or more)
        Seller's increment : Rs. 300
        Result             : MAX(1000, 300)  = Rs. 1,000

        The seller typed 300, but 300 is smaller than 1,000, so the
        seller's 300 is ignored. This is why the listing form shows 300
        while the live auction shows 1,000.

    Example 4  (seller wants a bigger step)
        Current price      : Rs. 8,000
        Price-based step   : Rs. 250
        Seller's increment : Rs. 1,000
        Result             : MAX(250, 1000)  = Rs. 1,000

        A seller can force a bigger step at any price.


6. THE NUMBER CHANGES DURING THE AUCTION
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The step is recalculated from the CURRENT price every time the page is
loaded or a bid is placed. So it can go up as bidding continues.

Walk-through (seller increment = Rs. 100):

    Starting bid (current price) . Rs. 4,500   -> step Rs. 100
        first bid must be at least 4,500 + 100 = Rs. 4,600
    Someone bids Rs. 4,900 ....... price 4,900 -> step Rs. 100
        next bid must be at least Rs. 5,000
    Someone bids Rs. 5,000 ....... price 5,000 -> step Rs. 250
        (price just reached the 5,000 line, so the step went up)
        next bid must be at least Rs. 5,250
    ... bidding continues ...
    Price reaches Rs. 20,000 ..... step becomes Rs. 500
    Price reaches Rs. 50,000 ..... step becomes Rs. 1,000 and stays there

Note: the step is chosen using the price BEFORE the new bid. So if the
price is Rs. 19,900 the step is still 250 and the next bid must be at
least Rs. 20,150. After that bid the price is above 20,000, so the
following step is 500.


7. THE FIRST BID
---------------------------------------------------------------------
When an auction has no bids yet, the "current price" is the seller's
STARTING BID. The first bid must still be at least:

    starting bid + step

So you cannot bid exactly the starting price. Example: starting bid
Rs. 20,000, step Rs. 500 -> the first bid must be at least Rs. 20,500.


8. PROXY (AUTOMATIC) BIDDING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
If a bidder sets a secret maximum (proxy bid), the site bids for them.
The step is used here too:

    price paid = the lower of
                 (leader's maximum)  and
                 (runner-up's maximum + step for the runner-up's amount)

So the leader always pays just ONE STEP above the second-highest bidder,
never more than their own maximum.


9. WHERE THE SITE CHECKS THIS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Display : the box "Minimum bid increase" on the live auction page
            (resources/views/livewire/user/auction-detail.blade.php).
- Calculate: Auction::getStepIncrement()  (app/Models/Auction.php) and
             AuctionEngineService::getStepIncrement()
             (app/Services/AuctionEngineService.php). Both use the same
             table and the same MAX rule.
- Enforce : when a bid is placed, AuctionEngineService::processBid()
            re-checks that the bid is at least current price + step.
            The check happens on the server, so it cannot be bypassed
            from the browser.


10. QUICK SUMMARY
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- It is CALCULATED, not fixed.
- It depends on the current price (table in section 3).
- It is never smaller than what the seller typed in "Min Bid Increment".
- It is the BIGGER of those two numbers.
- At Rs. 50,000 and above the price-based step is Rs. 1,000, so many
  expensive auctions show +Rs. 1,000.
- A seller's small number (like 300) has no effect once the price-based
  step is bigger.
